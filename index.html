ScanWais Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

ScanWais ("the app," "we," "us") is a grocery price-scanning and shopping-list app. This page explains what data the app collects, how it's used, and who it's shared with.

No account required

ScanWais does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide your name, email address, or phone number to use the app. We do not collect this information at all.

Data stored only on your device

The following information is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to us or to any third party:

  • Your shopping / to-buy list
  • Your cart items and scanned product prices
  • Your budget settings
  • Your saved receipts and shopping history

This data stays on your device and is removed if you delete the app. We have no access to it and cannot see, recover, or transfer it.

Camera use

ScanWais uses your device's camera to scan product barcodes and photograph price tags for on-device text recognition. Photos captured for this purpose are processed entirely on your device and are never saved, uploaded, or transmitted anywhere — they are discarded immediately after processing.

Subscriptions (RevenueCat)

ScanWais offers an optional premium subscription. Subscription purchases are processed through Apple's App Store / Google Play and managed using RevenueCat, a subscription management service. RevenueCat collects the following on our behalf to keep your subscription working correctly:

Data typePurposeUsed for tracking?
Purchase historyEnable and validate your premium subscriptionNo
An anonymous user IDIdentify your subscription recordNo
A device identifierFraud prevention and purchase validationNo

This data is not used for advertising, is never shared with data brokers or third-party ad networks, and is not combined with data from other companies' apps or websites. You can read RevenueCat's own privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Crash reporting (Sentry)

If the app crashes or encounters an unexpected error, ScanWais sends an anonymous crash report to Sentry, a crash-reporting service. This report contains the error details, app version, and device model and OS version. It does not contain your name, email address, shopping list, cart items, budget, or saved receipts. This data is not used for advertising or tracking, and is not combined with data from other companies' apps or websites. You can read Sentry's own privacy policy at sentry.io/privacy.

Report an issue

ScanWais lets you report a scan or shopping-list match issue from within the app. Doing so opens your device's own mail app with a pre-filled email — addressed to us, containing the scanned text and product details relevant to the issue, and any note you choose to add — for you to review before sending. Nothing is sent unless you tap send in your mail app.

Children's privacy

ScanWais is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
